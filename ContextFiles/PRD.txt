1. Executive Summary

Algorithmic Futures is a Python-based algorithmic trading system
engineered to pass the Topstep $50,000 Trading Combine. The system
deploys a regime-switched execution architecture: a Hidden Markov Model
(HMM) classifier determines the prevailing market state each evening,
and the appropriate strategy fires automatically the following session.

The core design philosophy treats the challenge as a constrained
survival problem, not a return-maximization contest. Every system
decision flows from a single constraint: reach a $3,000 profit target
without breaching a $2,000 Maximum Loss Limit (MLL).

  ---------------------- ------------------------------------------------

                         consistent payouts

  True Risk Capital      $2,000 (MLL)

  Profit Target          $3,000

  Execution Platform     TopstepX via ProjectX API (~$14.50/month)

  Instrument             MES (Micro E-mini S&P 500) — never full ES

  Risk Per Trade         $20–$40 (1–2% of MLL)

  Strategy B             15-Minute Opening Range Breakout (trending
                         regime)

  Regime Classifier      3-state Gaussian HMM with nightly walk-forward
                         refit

  Validation Method      Monte Carlo simulation — 10,000 trade-path runs
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

2. Problem Statement

2.1 The Challenge

Proprietary trading firms like Topstep operate a B-book model during
evaluations: the firm acts as direct counterparty to trades. Between
70–90% of retail traders fail evaluations, not due to a lack of
directional insight, but due to poor risk management, emotional trading,
and position sizing that is incompatible with strict drawdown
constraints.

The Topstep $50,000 combine imposes three simultaneous constraints that
most retail strategies cannot satisfy:

-   Drawdown ceiling: Never lose more than $2,000 (the MLL) at any point
    — account is terminated if breached

-   Profit target: Must reach $3,000 profit before timing out — forcing
    enough trading activity to create exposure

-   Consistency rule: No single trading day can account for more than
    50% of cumulative profit (40% for Express Funded payouts)

3. Goals & Non-Goals

3.1 Goals

-   G1: Pass the Topstep $50,000 Trading Combine with a probability of
    success > 60% per attempt (validated by Monte Carlo)

-   G2: Never breach the $2,000 MLL under any sequence of trade outcomes
    within validated parameters

-   G3: Operate fully autonomously during RTH session (9:30 ET – 3:05 PM
    CT) with zero manual intervention required

-   G4: Enforce all Topstep consistency and daily loss rules
    automatically via hardcoded circuit breakers

-   G5: Adapt strategy selection daily based on quantitative regime
    detection — never force a single strategy into incompatible
    conditions

-   G6: Complete the challenge in the minimum number of trading sessions
    to reduce subscription fee exposure

3.2 Non-Goals

-   NOT a goal: Maximize absolute return — survival and drawdown control
    take precedence over profit maximization

-   NOT a goal: Trade multiple instruments — the system exclusively
    trades MES, no NQ, YM, or other futures

-   NOT a goal: Operate overnight or in extended hours — all positions
    are closed by 3:05 PM CT each session

-   NOT a goal: Support manual discretionary overrides — the algorithm
    is fully systematic with no human intervention during trading hours

-   NOT a goal: Build a general-purpose trading platform — this system
    is purpose-built for the Topstep $50K combine constraints

-   NOT a goal: Predict market direction — the system exploits
    statistical tendencies, not directional forecasts

7.2 Data Flow: Nightly Refit

1.  APScheduler triggers nightly refit job at 17:30 CT

2.  session_manager.py calls yfinance (or equivalent) to fetch latest
    daily OHLCV + VIX/VIX3M bar

3.  indicators.py computes: log_return, atr_percentile (252-day rank),
    vix_term_slope

4.  hmm_classifier.py appends new feature row to expanding training
    window

5.  GaussianHMM.fit() runs on updated window (max HMM_LOOKBACK_DAYS
    bars)

6.  _map_states_to_regimes() re-sorts state labels by volatility to
    ensure consistency

7.  Predicted regime for t+1 written to state.json persistence file

8.  HMM diagnostics logged: transition matrix, state means, predicted
    regime label

7.3 Data Flow: Live Trading Session

9.  Pre-market init (08:30 CT): load regime from state.json; connect
    WebSocket; verify circuit breaker state

10. RTH open (09:30 ET): session_manager activates appropriate strategy
    module based on regime

11. market_data.py receives real-time tick stream via WebSocket callback

12. indicators.py updates VWAP, SD bands, CVD, and ATR on every new bar
    close

13. Strategy module evaluates entry conditions on each bar (every 5
    minutes)

14. If entry signal: order_manager.py calls circuit_breakers.check_all()
    — abort if any breaker active

15. position_sizer.py returns MES contract quantity (1 in most cases at
    $20-40 risk)

16. api_client.py dispatches order to ProjectX; awaits fill confirmation
    via WebSocket

17. On fill: exit orders placed; daily_pnl and trade_count updated;
    trade logged

18. At 3:05 PM CT: session_manager forces close_all_positions() via
    api_client; session summary logged

8.3 Paper Trading Acceptance

  Gate for Live Deployment

  The system must complete a minimum of 10 full paper-trading sessions
  (via ProjectX simulation mode) before any live combine attempt.

  ALL of the following must be true across all 10 sessions to proceed to
  live:

  • Zero MLL breaches

  • Zero consistency rule violations

  • Zero missed EOD closes (positions held past 3:05 PM CT)

  • Zero duplicate orders placed

  • All circuit breaker logs are present and match expected trigger
  conditions

  • Net simulated P&L is positive or within -$200 of breakeven

12. Glossary

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Term            Definition
  --------------- -------------------------------------------------------
  ATR             Average True Range — a measure of realized volatility
                  over N bars

  B-book          Execution model where the broker/firm acts as direct
                  counterparty to client trades

  Circuit Breaker Automated trading halt triggered when a P&L threshold
                  (loss or profit) is reached

  CVD             Cumulative Volume Delta — running sum of (buy volume -
                  sell volume); measures order flow pressure

  EOD             End of Day — Topstep's drawdown trail method; MLL only
                  trails from realized end-of-session highs

  Gambler's Ruin  Statistical theorem describing the probability of going
                  bankrupt with finite capital against infinite capital

  HMM             Hidden Markov Model — probabilistic model that infers
                  latent states from observable data

  MAE             Maximum Adverse Excursion — the largest unrealized loss
                  observed during an open trade

  MES             Micro E-mini S&P 500 futures contract ($1.25/tick,
                  1/10th the size of ES)

  MFE             Maximum Favorable Excursion — the largest unrealized
                  gain observed during an open trade

  MLL             Maximum Loss Limit — Topstep's drawdown threshold; true
                  operating capital of the account

  Monte Carlo     Statistical simulation method that randomizes trade
                  sequence to stress-test strategy survivability

  ORB             Opening Range Breakout — a momentum strategy using the
                  first N minutes of session as a range

  RTH             Regular Trading Hours — 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM ET for US
                  equity futures

  VIX Term Slope  (VIX3M - VIX) / VIX — positive = contango (calm);
                  negative = backwardation (stress)

  VWAP            Volume-Weighted Average Price — dynamic equilibrium
                  price weighted by actual volume traded

  Walk-Forward    Validation technique where the model is only trained on
                  historical data predating the prediction date
