Project Summary — Topstep Combine Survival Engine
Objective

Design a statistically robust intraday futures system (MES) capable of passing a Topstep $50K Combine under:

$2,000 trailing drawdown

$1,000 daily loss limit

Consistency rules

Sequence risk / variance clustering

Primary metric:

Probability of hitting +$3,000 before ruin within 20 trading days.

This is a survival optimization problem, not alpha maximization.

Current Strategy Architecture
Core Strategy: VWAP Mean Reversion (MR)

Entry:

Fade deviations from VWAP

Sigma-based threshold (tested 1.3–2.2σ)

Reclaim condition required

Attempt limits per side

Regime filter (range vs trend via ADX/ATR)

Exit:

VWAP target (primary)

Hard stop

Thesis-break MAE

Time-stop (removed in later experiments)

Runner/staged exits (tested, mostly failed)

Monte Carlo:

Block bootstrap by session

Multi-batch CI gate

Severe stress / mild stress / tilt stress scenarios

Day-horizon limit (20 days)
